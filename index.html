The method

The book is the standard. 1Close hears whether it was met.

The Easiest Sales Method on the Planet is the book. The method in it is Frame State Selling.

A buyer has to say a few things before a real yes. The problem, in their words. Why they have not already fixed it. Why now. Money, and who decides, are checks. They are not speeches.

The book is that standard, short enough for a closer to read. 1Close is how a founder hears whether the standard was met, on every call, without being on the call.

The work

Audit. Install. Coach the team.

01

Audit

We read the calls the team is already taking. Where a deal dies. What the closer did in that moment. What a better call would have asked.

You get the readout. The readout is the thing we stand behind.

02

Install

1Close goes onto those calls. It identifies the opportunity and puts the next action in front of the closer. It shows up on the call, in Slack, and in the inbox.

03

Coach the team

I coach the team on the gaps the calls revealed. The current install is four calls, across four weeks when the team is at normal volume.

Ongoing coaching for the team is optional. Coaching one closer, or taking the sales manager seat myself, is separate work.

After the coaching block

What stays on when I am not in the room

Help win the call

Live Coach is in the closer’s ear. It names what the buyer just said, and the move to make next.

Help progress the deal

After the call, the team gets a summary. In the morning, a briefing. A list of the deals the record reads as closable. A plain recap the closer can send to the buyer. A short brief before the next call.

Help improve the team

Once a week, the objections the team actually heard, and a report on each closer. Each closer gets one drill, taken from a moment on their own calls.

When the coaching block ends, this stays on. It reads the calls and prompts the next action.
